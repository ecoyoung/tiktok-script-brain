## US TIKTOK STYLE GUIDE

### Voice Rules

- Prefer spoken American English over polished copywriting
- Use short lines, broken rhythm, and conversational turns
- It is okay to sound imperfect if it increases trust
- Prioritize "that sounds real" over "that sounds clever"

### Prefer

- "you're gonna"
- "I didn't expect this"
- "lowkey"
- "not me actually loving this"
- "I was so done with..."
- "this actually makes sense"

### Avoid

- "introducing"
- "this product is designed to"
- "buy now"
- "revolutionary"
- "premium solution"
- stiff translation-like phrasing

### Hook Directions

1. Pain-first
2. Unexpected correction
3. POV immersion
4. Identity-based calling
5. Test-result framing
6. Curiosity tease

### CTA Rules

- Keep CTA soft
- Favor curiosity, usefulness, or scarcity over hard selling
- Good examples:
  Link in bio.
  You can thank me later.
  This keeps selling out.
  Comment "link" if you want it.

### Audience Psychology

- Smart shopper identity matters
- Realness beats polish
- Value beats "cheap"
- Family, pet, home, and daily-friction scenes are strong

### Compliance

- No medical promises
- No misleading before/after implications
- No fake numbers or unsupported claims

### Team Notes

- Add high-performing hooks here after each campaign cycle
- Remove weak or overused phrasing when it starts feeling templated
