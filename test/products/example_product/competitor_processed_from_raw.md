**Competitor Gap Analysis**  
- **Uncovered angle:** Most closet solutions focus on hanging systems, drawer dividers, or full-room makeovers. This micro-moment zeroes in on the exact second a stack collapses when pulling one item—a high-relatability detail competitors rarely isolate.  
- **Emotional gap:** Competitors often sell calm, aesthetic end-states but skip the repetitive, wasted-effort frustration. This script leverages the anger of “I just folded that!” and the quiet joy of it finally staying folded.  
- **Differentiation opportunity:** Position the product as “respect for your folding time.” Create content that dramatises the one-pull disaster with exaggerated thuds and ASMR re-stacking, then cuts to the serene stack reveal. This visceral contrast is something most tidy-home influencers miss, giving your creative a memorable edge.
