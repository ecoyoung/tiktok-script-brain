**Competitor Gap Analysis**  
- **Uncovered angle:** The script focuses mainly on function (stacks stay put), but never touches on aesthetics, small-space living, or the “mental weight” of visual clutter – angles that could deepen emotional resonance.  
- **Emotional gap:** The relief is described logically (“less chaotic”), but the creator doesn’t fully amplify the emotional transformation – how it feels to open a calm closet every morning, or how order saves mental energy for busy parents/students.  
- **Differentiation opportunity:** Create a character-driven series (e.g., “Closet Therapy”) that pairs the same organizer with a mini emotional story – maybe a working mom’s 30-second morning reset, or a college student’s dorm transformation – layering lifestyle aspiration on top of utility. This shifts the product from a mere shelf divider to a daily mood elevator.
