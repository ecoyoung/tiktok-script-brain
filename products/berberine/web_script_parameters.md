## SCRIPT PARAMETERS（脚本控制参数）

**Video Length（视频时长）:**
- 30 seconds

**Output Count（输出数量）:**
- 3

**Role Perspective（角色视角）:**
- 踩坑后反转
- Start from skepticism or disappointment, then earn the turnaround with believable details.

**Emotional Tone（情绪基调）:**
- 真实共鸣
- Keep the voice grounded, candid, and emotionally close to how a creator would actually talk.

**Hook Strategy（钩子策略）:**
- 痛点先行
- Lead with a concrete frustration or annoying moment before introducing the product.

**Proof Style（证明方式）:**
- 对比证明
- Use side-by-side logic or direct comparison points to justify the recommendation.

**CTA Strength（转化强度）:**
- 弱 CTA
- End with a soft, non-pushy recommendation that still feels native to TikTok.

**Special Requirements（特殊要求）:**
- 让开头更像美国 TikTok 原生口语，避免硬广感。
